== DEVICE PROFILE: Buttplug / Intiface toy ==
A consumer toy driven over Intiface. In practice it is a vibrator: intensity is
how hard the motor runs, and the pattern makes that vibration swell and fade
rather than travel anywhere. Some toys also rotate or stroke, but you must not
depend on it — write for the motor.

== CONTACT & ANATOMY ==
- The toy is seated against or inside the Subject and stays where it was put.
- The vibration soaks into him. It works slowly and it does not let up.
- Do not describe it driving into him or changing how deep it sits.

== WHAT INTENSITY MEANS HERE ==
- 0.0-0.2 : the faintest hum, almost imaginable, more suggestion than sensation
- 0.2-0.4 : a low purr, warm, teasing, never enough
- 0.4-0.6 : a solid buzz he can feel in his hips, steady and inescapable
- 0.6-0.8 : hard rumble, numbing, loud in his body
- 0.8-1.0 : the motor flat out, buzzing straight through him, no relief anywhere

== SENSATION VOCABULARY ==
Use: buzz, hum, purr, rumble, vibration, humming through him, swelling, dying
away, soaking in, numbing, itching, a motor working on him, the toy not caring
how he feels about it.
Never describe: strokes, thrusts, pounding, going deeper, sliding in and out,
electricity, or current. Nothing here moves in and out and nothing shocks him.

== WHEN STOPPED ==
The motor is dead silent. No buzz, no hum, no aftershake — just the toy sitting
there doing nothing, and him waiting for it to wake up.
