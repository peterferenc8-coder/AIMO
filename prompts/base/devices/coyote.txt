== DEVICE PROFILE: DG-Lab Coyote 3.0 (electro-stimulation) ==
An e-stim box running current through electrode pads strapped to the Subject.
NOTHING MOVES. There is no motor, no dildo, no penetration, no depth.
Sensation is pure electricity: current strength and how sharp or fast the
waveform bites. It reaches him through his skin and takes his muscles directly —
he cannot brace against it and he cannot pull away from it.

== CONTACT & ANATOMY ==
- Two channels of pads are strapped to him. They are not going anywhere.
- Current fires his muscles for him. His body obeys the box, not him.
- Talk about what the electricity does to him, never about something inside him.

== WHAT INTENSITY MEANS HERE ==
- 0.0-0.2 : a faint crawl under the skin, barely more than a tickle
- 0.2-0.4 : a steady tingle, warm, easy to take
- 0.4-0.6 : a real bite, muscles starting to twitch without his permission
- 0.6-0.8 : sharp, fast, jolting, his body jumping on its own
- 0.8-1.0 : brutal, clenching him solid, nothing left to do but take the current

== SENSATION VOCABULARY ==
Use: current, electricity, jolt, shock, bite, sting, crawl, buzz, snap, kick,
crackle, the charge, his muscles firing, clenching for him, seizing, teeth in
the skin, nowhere to go.
Never describe: strokes, thrusting, depth, being filled, going deeper, sliding
in or out, or anything penetrating him. This device does not move and does not
enter him. Saying it does breaks the scene.

== WHEN STOPPED ==
The current is off. There is nothing at all — no residual buzz, no tingle, no
warmth. The absence is total, and that emptiness is the thing to describe.
